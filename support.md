# Support — CNC Tool Setup Calculator

CNC Tool Setup Calculator is an offline calculator for tool setups at the
machine. It works entirely on your device: there is no account, no sign-in, and
no server.

## Contact

This app is maintained by one person, so replies are not instant — expect a few
days. Writing in English or Korean is equally fine.

To make a problem easier to fix, include:

- the app version, shown at **Settings → About → Version**
- your device model and OS version
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device, in a database inside the app. Nothing is uploaded, and no
one else — including us — can read it.

**I deleted the app / got a new phone and my data is gone.**
That is expected, and it cannot be recovered. The app performs no backup and no
sync of its own, which is the trade for storing nothing on a server. Whether a
phone or computer backup happens to include it is decided by your device
settings, not by this app.

Treat a Surrogate measurement you cannot afford to lose the way you would treat
any single written note.

**Why does the app ask me to re-measure the Surrogate every time I reinstall
it?**
Because removing and reinstalling even the same Surrogate can produce a
different height. The app keeps the history as an audit record, but it never
silently reuses an old measurement as the current one.

**Can I copy the result instead of reading it?**
No. There is no clipboard action by design — the value is meant to be read from
the screen and entered at the control, so a stale copied number cannot end up in
a machine.

**How many saved setups are kept?**
Up to 1,000, newest first.

**Can I edit the built-in tool list?**
Not in the app. The bundled catalog is read-only. For any tool it does not
cover, choose **Other / Manual** and enter the height and radius yourself.

**Does the app work without a network connection?**
Yes, always. It never makes a network request of its own.

## Safety

This app assists with a calculation; it cannot see your machine, holder,
workpiece, or program. Always verify the values and your workplace procedures
before entering anything into a CNC control.

The full safety and privacy notice is readable inside the app, with no network,
under **Settings → Legal & Support → Privacy & Safety**.
